### 2. State management — local state in `OutwardInwardSupplies`

Add `autofill31dSource` (string | null) and `autofill31dLoading` (boolean) state to `OutwardInwardSupplies`. Pass these to `getColumns()` to control the column render. This follows the existing pattern where `isEditEnabled` state is managed in the same component and passed to `getColumns()`.
